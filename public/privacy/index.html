Privacy Policy — Walkthrough Notes

Effective: October 5, 2026

Walkthrough Notes is a site-visit operations app: voice dictation for walkthrough notes, task lists, recurring checklists, visit history, and an optional link to a Walkthrough Ops console for reporting. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information stored on your device

  • Notes and dictation transcripts you create during visits.
  • Photos you attach to visits.
  • Tasks, checklists, locations, and visit history you create in the app.
  • Your Groq API key, if you enter one for AI summaries — it is kept on your device and only sent to Groq when you tap AI summarize.

This data stays on your device unless you choose to link the app to a Walkthrough Ops console.

Information shared when you link the app

If you connect the app to a Walkthrough Ops console (your own server), finalized visit notes and completed audit forms are transmitted to that server so they appear on your reporting dashboard. Your console account email and login credentials are used only to authenticate you with your console.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to dictate notes, including background dictation while the app is open in the background.
  • Camera / photos — to attach visit photos to your reports.
  • Internet — to reach your Walkthrough Ops console when linked, and to reach Groq only when you use AI summarize with your own key.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • Voice audio is processed by your device's speech recognition; we do not operate our own voice servers.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device and on your Walkthrough Ops console, both under your control. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. Console data can be deleted from the console's dashboard or by contacting support.
