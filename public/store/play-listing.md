## Full description
SiteWalkNotes is the ops execution app for multi-location teams — everything Zenput does, without the enterprise price tag.

DICTATE, DON'T TYPE
- Hands-free voice dictation while you walk the site — keeps listening in the background until you stop it
- Notes sort themselves into sections as you talk
- Attach photos to any visit, download a clean report when you're done

TASKS THAT GET DONE
- Assign tasks to a location and a person, with due dates and priority
- Overdue work turns red and floats to the top — nothing slips
- Filter by location to see exactly what a store owes you

CHECKLISTS WITH REAL ANSWERS
- Every item is answered Yes / No / N/A — not just a checkmark
- Build checklists item-by-item; schedule them daily, weekly, or monthly
- Each run gets a compliance score, so you can compare stores at a glance

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS, AUTOMATICALLY
- Answer "No" on any checklist or audit item and create a follow-up task in one tap
- Issues become assigned, dated, prioritized work — not forgotten notes

AUDITS FROM YOUR CONSOLE
- Build audit forms in the SiteWalkNotes Ops web console, publish them, and they appear in the app
- Completed forms are stored on the device AND pushed to your console
- Works fully offline; everything syncs when you're back online

DASHBOARD VISIBILITY
- Home screen shows overdue tasks, work due today, and completion % by location

Everything lives on your device until you choose to link it to your console. No ads, no tracking, no data sold.

## Data safety (for the Play Console form)
- Data collected: notes/text you write, photos you attach, voice transcripts (via speech recognition), account email (only when you link the app to your console)
- Data shared: with YOUR SiteWalkNotes Ops server when you link the app; with Groq only when you tap AI summarize using your own API key
- No ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, no data sold
- Permissions: Microphone (dictation), Camera (visit photos), Internet (console sync + optional AI summarize), Foreground service microphone (background dictation)
