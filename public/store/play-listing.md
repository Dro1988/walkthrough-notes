## Full description
Walkthrough Notes is the field companion for site-visit teams.

DICTATE, DON'T TYPE
- Hands-free voice dictation while you walk the site — keeps listening in the background
- Say "add task" mid-dictation to capture follow-ups without breaking stride
- Attach photos to any visit

RUN THE ROUTE
- Recurring checklists: daily, weekly, monthly
- Per-section condition ratings: Good, Needs attention, Critical
- Visit history and activity feed per location

SYNC TO THE DASHBOARD
- Link the app to your Walkthrough Ops console and finalized visits push automatically
- Fill out your team's audit forms right from the Audits tab — even with photos
- Works fully offline; syncs when you're back online

Everything lives on your device until you choose to link it. No ads, no tracking.

## Data safety (for the Play Console form)
- Data collected: notes/text you write, photos you attach, voice transcripts (via on-device speech recognition), account email (only when you link the app)
- Data shared: with YOUR Walkthrough Ops server when you link the app; with Groq only when you tap AI summarize using your own API key
- No ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, no data sold
- Permissions: Microphone (dictation), Camera (visit photos), Internet (console sync + optional AI summarize), Foreground service microphone (background dictation)
